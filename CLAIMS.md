# Claims Ledger (`zero-error-torus-frontier` v1.0.0)

Every substantive mathematical and computational claim in this repository and its companion manuscript is indexed below with an explicit epistemic verification class. This ledger was corrected on 2026-11-26 to match an independent statement audit ([audit log](https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md)); the frozen paper still contains the earlier wording, and the differences are listed in [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md).

- **`[LEAN-6K]`** — Formally proved in zero-Mathlib Lean 4.35.0 (`lean/`), zero `sorry` in proved modules (the 18 intentional `sorry` sit only in the statement-only stub `PalomarChallenge.lean`), zero `native_decide`, zero custom axioms, axiom set $\subseteq \{\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}\}$, replayed across six independent proof kernels (`lean`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `lean4lean`, `nanoda`, `con-leche`, `con-ron` via `lake check --paranoid` and `lake comparator --paranoid`).
- **`[EXACT-COMP]`** — Checked by exact integer / rational / $\mathbb{F}_2$ arithmetic in Rust (`rust/`), ISO C11 (`native/`), and Python (`python/`, `reference/`), not in Lean.
- **`[OPEN]`** — Explicitly open problem NOT claimed solved.

Shannon capacity $\Theta$ is not defined in Lean. Every statement "$\Theta(G) \ge a$" or "$\Theta(G) = b$" below is a consequence, outside Lean, of the integer statements that Lean proves, via $\Theta(G) = \sup_d \alpha(G^{\boxtimes d})^{1/d}$.

No claim in this ledger resolves a published open problem or conjecture.

## What Would Falsify or Refute These Claims

Every claim in this ledger is falsifiable by a concrete, publicly executable check:

1. **Refuting any `[LEAN-6K]` theorem (`C-1a`–`C-4d`):**
   - Demonstrating any forbidden axiom (`sorryAx`, `Lean.ofReduceBool`, `Lean.trustCompiler`, or any custom axiom) in `lake env lean VerifyAxioms.lean`.
   - Triggering a rejection by any of the six independent proof kernels (`lean`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `lean4lean`, `nanoda`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`) under `lake check --paranoid` or `lake comparator --config comparator.json --paranoid`.
   - Exhibiting a semantic discrepancy between any Lean statement in `lean/Challenge.lean` and the prose in this ledger. Known differences between the Lean statements and the frozen paper are listed in `docs/statement-fidelity.md` and `ERRATA.md`.
2. **Refuting any certified lower bound (`C-2a`–`C-2d`):**
   - Exhibiting a conflicting pair of vertices in any base packing (`Q25_HBPZ`, `Q29_HBPZ`, `Q25_HStar`, Baumert pullbacks) or any port/footprint witness in `data/witnesses.json`.
   - Showing that the certified 15-decimal integer target $t$ violates $t^p \le M \cdot (10^{15})^p$ in exact arbitrary-precision arithmetic (`reference/capacity_oracle.py`).
3. **Refuting empirical claim `C-EX1`:**
   - Exhibiting an independent 2D subgroup $H(a,b,c,d) \le \mathbb{Z}_n^4$ ($n \in \{19, 21, 23, 25\}$) with quotient ball size $|B_Q(H)| < 45$.
4. **Comparison with the literature (not a refutation of the Lean statements):**
   - The bounds C-2b and C-2d are already below published values (Codenotti–Gerace–Resta 2003, Table 1). A published bound above C-2a or C-2c, for example in sources not opened in the 2026-11-26 search (Baumert et al. 1971, Hales 1973, or later work citing the 2003 paper), would show that these two are not best known either, without falsifying the machine-checked inequalities.
