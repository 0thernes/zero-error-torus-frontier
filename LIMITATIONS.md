# Limitations and Threats to Validity

This document records explicit mathematical, formalization, and computational boundaries of `zero-error-torus-frontier` (`v1.0.0`).

## 1. What Is NOT Solved

1. **Exact Shannon Capacity of Odd Cycles $C_n$ ($n \ge 7$):** Determining the exact value of $\Theta(C_n)$ for any odd $n \ge 7$ (Mega-Atlas `CS-049`) remains a major open problem in combinatorics and zero-error information theory. Our results establish strictly stronger lower bounds for $C_{25}, C_{27}, C_{29}, C_{31}$ and infinite exact-ceiling $d=4$ quotient families, not matching upper bounds (Lovász's $\vartheta(C_{25}) \approx 12.4505$ remains above our $12.344196$).
2. **Existing 2026 Lower Bounds for $C_7, C_{11}, C_{13}, C_{15}, C_{19}, C_{21}, C_{23}$:** We do NOT improve the live 2026 records of Tandon (`arXiv:2608.30273`, $C_7 \ge 3.25883262$) or Buys–Polak–Zuiddam (`arXiv:2607.29681` / `shannon-capacity-lean`, $C_{11}$–$C_{23}$). Rather, Theorem T1c proves the exact Intra-Coset Obstruction explaining why BPZ's $Q_{21}$ carries zero ports, and our independent local-search reproduction on $C_{23}$ confirms BPZ's 50-port $(0,-1,-1,-1)$ shift sits right at the MIS ceiling of the 208 Baumert-pullback private pairs.
3. **Regime $n \ge 33$ ($d \ge 5$):** At $n = 33 = 2^5 + 1$, the classical $d=5$ Baumert linear code gives $\Theta(C_{33}) \ge 33^{4/5} \approx 16.398765$, exceeding every $d=4$ coset bound ($\le (66 \cdot 33^2)^{1/4} \approx 16.373554$). Accordingly, our new all-time lower-bound records lie strictly in the $d=4$ frontier window $25 \le n \le 31$, while our infinite exact-ceiling theorems T1d and T1e prove sharp optimal density within the $d=4$ quotient classes for all $m \ge 5$ ($m \equiv 0,1 \pmod 4$) and all $k \ge 3$ ($n = 8k+1$).
4. **Congruence Class $m \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ in $H_{\mathrm{BPZ}}$:** Our closed-form 1-line helical formula for $H_{\mathrm{BPZ}}$ achieves the exact ceiling for all $m \equiv 0,1 \pmod 4$ ($C_{21}, C_{33}, C_{37}, C_{49}, \dots$), and $H^*$ achieves it for all even $m = 2k$ ($m \equiv 0,2 \pmod 4$: $C_{25}, C_{33}, C_{41}, \dots$). For $m \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ (such as $C_{29}$, $m=7$, $M=50$), we provide an explicit verified 50-coset packing rather than a closed-form infinite formula.

## 2. Formalization & TCB Boundaries

1. **Integer Power Statements vs. Real Supremum Limit:** Because the Lean 4.35.0 formalization has **zero Mathlib dependency**, capacity lower bounds are stated in their strictly stronger, exact integer form: existence of an explicit independent set $I \subseteq G^{\boxtimes p}$ with $a^p \le |I| \cdot (10^{15})^p$, which immediately implies $\Theta(G) = \sup_{d \ge 1} \alpha(G^{\boxtimes d})^{1/d} \ge a / 10^{15}$ by elementary monotonicity of $x \mapsto x^{1/p}$ on $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$. Similarly, upper bounds are stated as exact universal integer inequalities $\alpha(G^{\boxtimes d}) \le B^d$ (and $\alpha(C_5^{\boxtimes d})^2 \le 5^d$) for all $d \ge 1$.
2. **Local Windows vs. Linux Six-Kernel Replay:** On Windows, `lean4lean.exe` is not bundled in the standard Elan toolchain, and `lake comparator` requires Linux `bubblewrap`. Full 6-kernel replay (`lean`, `leanchecker`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `con-leche --verified`, `con-ron --verified`, `nanoda_bin`) and `lake comparator --paranoid` are executed automatically on Ubuntu CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`), while `lean`, `leanchecker`, and `leanchecker-paranoid` are additionally verified locally on Windows.
3. **Peer-Review Status:** This work is an independent preprint and formal verification artifact; it has **not** undergone external journal peer review. All formal claims are backed instead by multi-kernel machine proofs and independent multi-language verification.

## 3. Literature Baseline Caveat

Baseline comparisons for $C_{25}, C_{27}, C_{29}, C_{31}$ are evaluated against the complete Baumert–McEliece–Rodemich–Rumsey–Stanley–Taylor (1971) / Hales (1973) $d \in \{2,3,4,5\}$ linear-code and expansion bounds (where $d=3$ dominates $d=4$ on $C_{25..31}$: $1875^{1/3} = 12.3310604178$ for $C_{25}$, $2362^{1/3} = 13.3176204$ for $C_{27}$, $2927^{1/3} = 14.3045513$ for $C_{29}$, $3575^{1/3} = 15.2906289$ for $C_{31}$) and live November 2026 arXiv/GitHub records (Itty et al. `2607.21517`, Gao `2607.27869`, Buys–Polak–Zuiddam `2607.29681`, Tandon `2608.30273`). If an unpublished or unindexed lower bound for $C_{25..31}$ exceeds these figures, it will be documented immediately in `ERRATA.md`.
