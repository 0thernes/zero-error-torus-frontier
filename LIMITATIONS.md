# Limitations and Threats to Validity

This document records explicit mathematical, formalization, and computational boundaries of `zero-error-torus-frontier` (`v1.0.0`).

## 1. What Is NOT Solved

1. **Exact Shannon Capacity of Odd Cycles $C_n$ ($n \ge 7$):** Determining the exact value of $\Theta(C_n)$ for any odd $n \ge 7$ (Mega-Atlas `CS-049`) remains a major open problem in combinatorics and zero-error information theory. Our results are lower bounds, not matching upper bounds (Lovász's $\vartheta(C_{25}) \approx 12.4505$ remains above our $12.344196$). Only the bounds for $C_{25}$ and $C_{29}$ exceed the largest published values we found; the bounds for $C_{27}$ and $C_{31}$ are below the values of Codenotti, Gerace and Resta (Ars Combin. 66, 2003, Table 1). The two $d=4$ families meet the quotient ceiling of T1b, which bounds unions of cosets of one subgroup, not $\alpha(C_n^{\boxtimes 4})$.
2. **No Published Open Question Is Resolved:** Theorem T3c is a two-alphabet form of the BPZ combining rule (Tandon, arXiv:2608.30273, Proposition 1; Buys–Polak–Zuiddam, `Realisation.multiSubst`). It does not address the two-sided extension of Tandon's Theorem 1 that his Section 5 and Remark 3 leave to future work; earlier versions of this repository wrongly called that an "Open Problem #2" and claimed to resolve it.
3. **Existing 2026 Lower Bounds for $C_7, C_{11}, C_{13}, C_{15}, C_{19}, C_{21}, C_{23}$:** We do NOT improve the 2026 bounds of Tandon (`arXiv:2608.30273`, $C_7 \ge 3.25883262$) or Buys–Polak–Zuiddam (`arXiv:2607.29681` / `shannon-capacity-lean`, $C_{11}$–$C_{23}$). Rather, Theorem T1c proves one branch of an intra-coset obstruction ($e_3 - 2e_0 = \pm 1$, $n \ge 9$) relevant to why BPZ's $Q_{21}$ carries zero ports, and our independent local-search reproduction on $C_{23}$ confirms BPZ's 50-port $(0,-1,-1,-1)$ shift sits right at the MIS ceiling of the 208 Baumert-pullback private pairs.
4. **Regime $n \ge 33$ ($d \ge 5$):** At $n = 33 = 2^5 + 1$, the classical $d=5$ Baumert linear code gives $\Theta(C_{33}) \ge 33^{4/5} \approx 16.398765$, exceeding every $d=4$ coset bound ($\le (66 \cdot 33^2)^{1/4} \approx 16.373554$). Our lower bounds therefore concern only the window $25 \le n \le 31$. Theorems T1d and T1e show that the quotient ceiling of T1b is attained for all $m \ge 5$ ($m \equiv 0,1 \pmod 4$) and all $k \ge 3$ ($n = 8k+1$); many of these sizes were already published (see `CLAIMS.md`, C-1d and C-1e).
5. **Congruence Class $m \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ in $H_{\mathrm{BPZ}}$:** Our closed-form 1-line helical formula for $H_{\mathrm{BPZ}}$ achieves the exact ceiling for all $m \equiv 0,1 \pmod 4$ ($C_{21}, C_{33}, C_{37}, C_{49}, \dots$), and $H^*$ achieves it for all even $m = 2k$ ($m \equiv 0,2 \pmod 4$: $C_{25}, C_{33}, C_{41}, \dots$). For $m \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ (such as $C_{29}$, $m=7$, $M=50$), we provide an explicit verified 50-coset packing rather than a closed-form infinite formula.

## 2. Formalization & TCB Boundaries

1. **Integer Power Statements vs. Real Supremum Limit:** Because the Lean 4.35.0 formalization has **zero Mathlib dependency**, capacity lower bounds are stated in their strictly stronger, exact integer form: existence of an explicit independent set $I \subseteq G^{\boxtimes p}$ with $a^p \le |I| \cdot (10^{15})^p$, which immediately implies $\Theta(G) = \sup_{d \ge 1} \alpha(G^{\boxtimes d})^{1/d} \ge a / 10^{15}$ by elementary monotonicity of $x \mapsto x^{1/p}$ on $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$. Similarly, upper bounds are stated as exact universal integer inequalities $\alpha(G^{\boxtimes d}) \le B^d$ (and $\alpha(C_5^{\boxtimes d})^2 \le 5^d$) for all $d \ge 1$. $\Theta$, real numbers and $\sqrt{5}$ are not defined in Lean; every statement about $\Theta$ is a consequence outside Lean.
2. **Local Windows vs. Linux Six-Kernel Replay:** On Windows, `lean4lean.exe` is not bundled in the standard Elan toolchain, and `lake comparator` requires Linux `bubblewrap`. Full 6-kernel replay (`lean`, `leanchecker`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `con-leche --verified`, `con-ron --verified`, `nanoda_bin`) and `lake comparator --paranoid` are executed automatically on Ubuntu CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`), while `lean`, `leanchecker`, and `leanchecker-paranoid` are additionally verified locally on Windows.
3. **Peer-Review Status:** This work is an independent preprint and formal verification artifact; it has **not** undergone external journal peer review. All formal claims are backed instead by multi-kernel machine proofs and independent multi-language verification.
4. **Scope of the Lean Contract:** The 18-target comparator contract (`comparator.json`) and `VerifyAxioms.lean` do not include `WorldRecords.hstarOddRichPortSystem`, `hstarOddRichPortSystem_sz`, `CosetQuotient.baumertExpandList_len` or `baumertExpandList_nodup_and_indep`. These are kernel-checked on the three standard axioms, but the port system of T1e and the cardinality formula of T1f are not part of the contract statements. Several contract statements are narrower than the frozen paper (T1c, T4b); see `docs/statement-fidelity.md` and `ERRATA.md`.

## 3. Literature Baseline Caveat

The v1.0.0 release compared its bounds against third-power values ($1875^{1/3}$, $2362^{1/3}$, $2927^{1/3}$, $3575^{1/3}$) and stated that $d=3$ dominates $d=4$ on $C_{25..31}$. That was wrong. The best published values we found are the $d = 4$ entries of Codenotti, Gerace and Resta, *Some remarks on the Shannon capacity of odd cycles*, Ars Combinatoria 66 (2003) 243–257, Table 1: $\alpha(C_n^{\boxtimes 4}) \ge 23125, 31522, 42017, 54934$ for $n = 25, 27, 29, 31$. Against these, the margins are $+0.012567519$ ($C_{25}$), $-0.005186$ ($C_{27}$), $+0.002810330$ ($C_{29}$) and $-0.000699$ ($C_{31}$). The same table gives third-power values $2929$ ($C_{29}$) and $3580$ ($C_{31}$), not $2927$ and $3575$; arXiv:2506.14654, Theorem 1.7, gives $3576$ for $C_{31}^{\boxtimes 3}$. The correction is logged in `ERRATA.md` (2026-11-26).

Search scope (2026-11-26): CGR 2003 Table 1; arXiv:2607.29681v1 and the Buys–Polak–Zuiddam Lean repository (bounds stop at $C_{23}$); arXiv:2607.21517, 2608.30273, 2404.16763, 2509.24600 and Korze–Vesel (Ars Combin. 106), which contain no value for $C_{25}$–$C_{31}$. Not opened: Baumert et al. (1971) and Hales (1973). The 2003 table entries are printed cardinalities; the explicit sets were not examined. The search was not exhaustive, so the $C_{25}$ and $C_{29}$ bounds are "above the largest published values found", not established records.
