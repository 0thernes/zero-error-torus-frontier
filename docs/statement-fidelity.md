# Statement Fidelity Audit (`zero-error-torus-frontier` v1.0.0)

This table lists, per theorem, the exact Lean statement and every known difference from the prose in [`paper/main.tex`](../paper/main.tex), [`CLAIMS.md`](../CLAIMS.md) and [`README.md`](../README.md). The Lean statements are in [`lean/Challenge.lean`](../lean/Challenge.lean) and are proved in [`lean/Solution.lean`](../lean/Solution.lean). The table was corrected on 2026-11-26 after an independent statement audit ([audit log](https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md)); the earlier version wrongly asserted a literal one-to-one match. Paper-side corrections are listed in [`ERRATA.md`](../ERRATA.md).

Shannon capacity $\Theta$, real numbers and $\sqrt{5}$ are not defined in Lean. Every statement about $\Theta$ in the prose is a consequence, outside Lean, of the integer statements below.
